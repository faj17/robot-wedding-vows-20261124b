One original funny line of wedding vows for a robot couple (151 characters):

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded unit, to have and to hold, in sickness and in low battery, until death do us part — or until our warranties expire.

The setup is the classic wedding-vow cadence, robot-flavoured: "lawfully wedded unit", "in sickness and in low battery". The punchline undercuts the vow's most solemn line at the very last word — the marriage does not end at death, it ends when the warranties run out. One sentence, a real promise in it, the surprise lands on the last word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
